        \emph{Note}. The original and the reproduction result can be compared for identity or consistency (see section "Discussion and Recommendations"). The size of disks indicates the importance of various reasons for differing results. A small disk---with circle (\faIcon[regular]{dot-circle}) and without (\bullet)---indicates that there is no difference or that a reason is less important. A large disk ( \faCircle) indicates a potentially important reason for non-identical or inconsistent results (but we do not know which one, if a row contains more than one). A circle around a small disk (\faIcon[regular]{dot-circle}) indicates that a reason is possible, but the caused difference is probably not larger than for reasons with a large disk. The equal sign (=) indicates that the phase containing this reason is inherited and, therefore, assumed to be identical. Therefore, it cannot be responsible for differences in results by itself, though issues in that phase cannot be detected.
